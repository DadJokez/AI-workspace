**Callout** — inline notice block; the calm alternative to a toast or banner. Tinted surface + accent bar.

```jsx
<Callout tone="accent" title="Heads up" icon={<Icon name="info" />}>
  Committed-use pricing applies after 100k DBU/month.
</Callout>
<Callout tone="danger">Your token expired. Re-authenticate to continue.</Callout>
```

Tones: `neutral | accent | info | success | warning | danger`.
