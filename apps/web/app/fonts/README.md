# Comparative webfonts

These WOFF2 assets are bundled by `next/font/local`; the production app does
not request fonts from a third-party host.

## Provenance

- `Geist-Variable.woff2` and `GeistMono-Variable.woff2` come from
  [`vercel/geist-font`](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) at commit
  `10dc7658f13c38a474cde201bb09a4617267545b`.
- `Newsreader-Regular.woff2` and `Newsreader-Italic.woff2` are the 16pt optical
  masters from [`productiontype/Newsreader`](https://github.com/productiontype/Newsreader)
  at commit `cfcb4f7af0e52c25e8df2a2431814c8e5fe2e155`.

Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Exact
license texts are committed in [`licenses`](./licenses).
